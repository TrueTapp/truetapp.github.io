How It Works

Pick a photo. That's it.

Choose a Photo

Snap a new photo or pick one from your library. A sunset, your pet, a concert — any moment works. That's the only required step.

Add Context (Optional)

Want more control? Add keywords to guide the AI — a name, a feeling, a memory. The song becomes even more personal and accurate.

Listen & Share

Your unique song with original lyrics and vocals is ready in minutes. Play it, read the lyrics, and share it anywhere.

Features

More than you'd expect

Two Versions Per Song

Every photo generates two unique song versions — different styles, different vibes. Pick your favorite or keep both.

Smart Suggestions

The app learns your style over time. It suggests fresh ideas, detects repetitive patterns, and recommends new genres — all privately on your device, powered by Apple Intelligence.

Live Activity & Dynamic Island

Watch your song being created in real-time on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. You'll know exactly when it's ready.

Home Screen Widgets

Your latest songs and generation status live right on your Home Screen. Quick access without opening the app.
