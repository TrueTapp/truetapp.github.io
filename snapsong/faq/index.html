SnapSong

Frequently Asked Questions

General

What is SnapSong?

SnapSong is an app that turns any photo into an original song with vocals, lyrics, and custom cover art. Just pick a photo — from your camera or library — and the app creates a unique song inspired by the image, in minutes.

How does it work?

SnapSong uses AI to analyze your photo, understand the scene and mood, write original lyrics, and compose a full song with vocals. The process takes a few minutes and happens automatically — no musical knowledge required.

Do I need any musical experience?

Not at all. SnapSong is designed for everyone. You don't need to play an instrument, read music, or know anything about music production. Just choose a photo and the app does the rest.

What kind of photos work best?

Any photo works — sunsets, pets, people, food, concerts, landscapes, everyday moments. The AI analyzes the visual elements, colors, mood, and context to create a song that matches. More visually expressive photos tend to produce more interesting results.

Songs & Generation

How long does it take to generate a song?

Most songs are ready in 2 to 5 minutes. You can follow the progress in real-time through the Live Activity on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.

Why do I get two versions of each song?

Every photo generates two unique song versions with different styles, tempos, and vibes. This gives you more variety — you can keep your favorite or enjoy both.

What are keywords and how do they help?

Keywords are optional words or phrases you can add before generating a song — like a name, a feeling, a memory, or a musical style. They guide the AI to create a more personalized and accurate result. If you don't add any, the AI will interpret the photo on its own.

Can I choose the audio quality?

Yes. SnapSong lets you choose between standard and high-quality audio generation. Higher quality takes slightly longer but produces richer, more detailed sound.

Can I see the lyrics of my songs?

Yes. Every generated song comes with full lyrics that you can read in the song detail screen.

Can I share my songs?

Yes. You can share your songs using the standard iOS share sheet — send them via Messages, WhatsApp, email, AirDrop, or any other app.

Pricing & Subscription

Is SnapSong free?

SnapSong is free to download and includes a limited number of free song generations. To generate unlimited songs and access premium features, you can subscribe to SnapSong Premium.

What does the Premium subscription include?

SnapSong Premium gives you unlimited song generations, access to high-quality audio, and priority processing. Subscription options and pricing are shown in the app.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, anytime. You can manage or cancel your subscription through your Apple ID settings. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.

How do I restore my purchase?

If you reinstall the app or switch devices, your subscription is automatically restored when you sign in with the same Apple ID. You can also tap "Restore Purchases" in the app settings.

Privacy & Data

What happens to my photos?

Your photos are sent to AI services (OpenAI) for analysis and lyric generation, but they are not stored on any server. The original photo and the generated song are saved locally on your device only.

Where are my songs stored?

All songs, cover artwork, and original photos are stored locally on your device. SnapSong does not sync to the cloud or require an account.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. You can delete any song within the app at any time, which removes the audio, cover art, and original photo from your device. Uninstalling the app removes all SnapSong data.

What is the Creative Intelligence Engine?

It's a feature powered by Apple Foundation Models that runs entirely on your device. It analyzes your song history to suggest fresh keywords, detect repetitive patterns, and recommend new genres — all privately, with no data sent to the cloud.

Languages & Platforms

What languages are supported?

SnapSong generates songs and lyrics in 5 languages: English, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish, French, and German. The language is automatically detected from your device settings.

What devices are supported?

SnapSong is available on iPhone, iPad, Mac with Apple Silicon, and Apple Vision Pro. It requires iOS 26 or later.

Can I use Siri to create songs?

Yes. SnapSong supports Siri and Apple Shortcuts. You can say "Hey Siri, create a song" or build custom shortcuts for hands-free music creation.

Are there widgets?

Yes. SnapSong includes Home Screen widgets that show your latest songs and current generation status, so you can stay updated without opening the app.

Troubleshooting

My song generation failed. What should I do?

Song generation may occasionally fail due to network issues or high server demand. You can retry the generation from the song detail screen. Make sure you have a stable internet connection.

Why is generation taking longer than usual?

Generation time depends on server load and your internet connection. High-quality audio takes slightly longer. If it's been more than 10 minutes, try canceling and retrying.
