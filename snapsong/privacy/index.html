Privacy Policy

Effective: April 9, 2026

Last updated: April 9, 2026

Introduction

SnapSong is operated by TrueTapp – Mobile Apps Ltda, a company based in Brazil. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the measures we take to protect your privacy. SnapSong is designed with privacy as a core principle: your photos and generated songs are processed on your device first, and we only send necessary data to third-party AI services when required to generate your music.

Information We Collect

User Content

When you use SnapSong, you provide photos by capturing them with your device camera or selecting them from your library. These photos are processed to generate songs. The resulting song audio files, cover artwork, and original photos are stored locally on your device using SwiftData. No data leaves your device without your explicit action.

Usage Data

We collect anonymous usage data about how you interact with the app, including feature usage patterns, session duration, and generation history. This data is not linked to your identity and helps us understand how the app is used to improve its performance and user experience.

Diagnostics

Crash logs, performance metrics, and other diagnostic information are collected automatically by Apple's systems. This data is not linked to your personal identity and is used to identify and fix technical issues.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the SnapSong service and generate personalized songs from your photos
  • To improve the app's features, performance, and user experience
  • To identify, troubleshoot, and fix technical issues and bugs
  • To communicate with you about important updates or changes to the service (if you contact us)

Third-Party Services

OpenAI

Your photos are sent to OpenAI's API for visual analysis and lyric generation. OpenAI processes this data according to their API data usage policy and does not use API data to train or improve their models. For more details, consult OpenAI's API Data Usage Policy.

Suno

The generated lyrics and style parameters are sent to Suno's API for music generation. Suno creates the audio file based on this information. For details on how Suno handles your data, please review Suno's Privacy Policy.

Apple

SnapSong uses Apple's built-in analytics and crash reporting systems through the App Store. Additionally, the Creative Intelligence Engine leverages Apple Foundation Models that run entirely on your device. Your musical history and preferences never leave your iPhone. For more information, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

Important Note

SnapSong does not sell your data to any third parties. We do not use advertising SDKs or share your information with advertisers.

Data Storage & Security

All user content—including songs, photos, and cover artwork—is stored locally on your device. SnapSong does not require user accounts, does not sync data to cloud servers, and does not maintain centralized databases of your content. This design ensures your music remains entirely under your control.

We implement industry-standard security practices to protect data in transit when communicating with third-party APIs. However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure. We encourage you to use strong device passcodes and keep your iOS operating system up to date.

On-Device Intelligence

SnapSong's Creative Intelligence Engine uses Apple Foundation Models, which run entirely on your device. Your musical history, preferences, and content are processed locally and never transmitted to external servers for analysis or training purposes.

Data Retention

Data stored on your device persists until you explicitly delete songs or uninstall the app. When you delete a song within SnapSong, all associated files (audio, cover art, and original photo) are removed from your device. Uninstalling the app removes all local SnapSong data from your device.

Requests sent to third-party APIs (OpenAI and Suno) are processed immediately and not stored by SnapSong. These services may retain data according to their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing their policies for details on their data retention practices.

Children's Privacy

SnapSong does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, please contact us immediately at snapsong@truetapp.com so we can take appropriate action.

Your Rights

  • You have the right to delete any song and its associated data at any time directly within the SnapSong app
  • You can remove all SnapSong data from your device by uninstalling the app
  • You can access your own content (songs, photos) that is stored on your device at any time
  • If you have questions about your data or wish to exercise your rights, contact us at snapsong@truetapp.com

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy. Your continued use of SnapSong after any changes indicates your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
