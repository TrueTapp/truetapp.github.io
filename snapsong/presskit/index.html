How It Works

Pick a photo. That's it.

Choose a Photo

Snap a new photo or pick one from your library. A sunset, your pet, a concert — any moment works. That's the only required step.

Add Context (Optional)

Want more control? Add keywords to guide the AI — a name, a feeling, a memory. The song becomes even more personal and accurate.

Listen & Share

Your unique song with original lyrics and vocals is ready in minutes. Play it, read the lyrics, and share it anywhere.

Features

Built natively for iOS 26

Apple Intelligence

Powered by Apple Intelligence and built on Apple Foundation Models running locally on your iPhone. The model analyzes your song history — lyrics, keywords, styles — to suggest fresh directions, detect repetitive patterns, and recommend unexplored genres. No server calls, no cloud processing. All private, all on your device.

Live Activity & Dynamic Island

Watch your song being created in real-time on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island with ActivityKit.

Home Screen Widgets

Your latest songs and generation status live right on your Home Screen with WidgetKit.

Siri & Shortcuts

"Hey Siri, create a song." Full App Intents integration for hands-free music creation.

5 Languages

Songs and lyrics generated in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, and German — auto-detected from your device language.

Liquid Glass Design

Designed with the iOS 26 Liquid Glass visual language. Translucent materials, fluid animations, and depth throughout every screen.

Powered by Apple Intelligence

Creative Intelligence Engine

Built on Apple Foundation Models — a language model that runs entirely on your iPhone. It learns from your musical history to make every song more personal, with zero data sent to the cloud.

Smarter Keyword Suggestions

Using Apple Foundation Models on-device, SnapSong analyzes your previously generated lyrics, keywords, and musical styles to suggest fresh, creative directions — so every new song starts from a more inspired place.

Pattern Detection

The on-device model detects when you're falling into repetitive patterns — similar tones, recurring themes, overused verses — and nudges you toward unexplored territory, keeping your music diverse and authentic.

Genre & Style Discovery

Recommends musical genres and styles you haven't tried yet — based on what works well with your photos and preferences — turning every session into a fresh discovery.

Everything runs privately on-device. Your songs, your history, your data — never leaves your iPhone.

Inclusive by Design

Accessibility

SnapSong was built to be usable by everyone, from day one. Not as an afterthought — as a foundation.

VoiceOver

Every screen, button, and song card is fully labeled and navigable. Users who are blind or low-vision can create, browse, and play songs with full autonomy.

Voice Control

Navigate and interact with the entire app using only your voice. Create songs, browse your library, and control playback — completely hands-free.

Larger Text

All text scales seamlessly with the user's preferred size — from extra small to the largest accessibility sizes. Layouts adapt without breaking, nothing gets cut off.

Dark Interface

A native dark design that's easy on the eyes in any lighting. Optimized contrast and colors that look great on OLED displays while reducing eye strain.

Sufficient Contrast

Every element meets or exceeds contrast ratio guidelines. Text, icons, and interactive elements are clearly visible for users with low vision or color sensitivity.

Reduced Motion

For users sensitive to motion, all animations gracefully fade instead of sliding or bouncing. The experience stays polished without triggering discomfort.

Privacy First

Your Data Stays Yours

All data collected is not linked to your identity. Songs, photos, and personal data are stored locally on your device.

User Content

Photos and songs you create may be processed to generate music, but are never linked to your identity or shared with third parties.

Usage Data

Anonymous usage patterns help us improve the app experience. No personal information is attached — we see trends, not individuals.

Diagnostics

Crash logs and performance data help us fix bugs faster. Collected automatically by Apple, never linked to you personally.

The Story

My name is Henrique Velloso, and SnapSong was born from a simple idea: what if every photo had a soundtrack? We take hundreds of photos — of sunsets, road trips, people we love, random beautiful moments — but they stay silent. I wanted to change that.

As a 3x WWDC Scholarship winner (2015, 2017, 2018) and Apple Developer Academy alumnus (BEPiD 2015, one of the first cohorts worldwide), I've been building for Apple platforms for over a decade. One of the core lessons from the Academy has always stayed with me: build something that solves a real problem or makes someone's life easier. With SnapSong, I'm applying exactly that — making music creation accessible to anyone, even people who've never touched an instrument or read a single note.

SnapSong is not a tool for musicians — it's for everyone. Your mom at a birthday party. Your friend on a road trip. A kid pointing the camera at their dog. Anyone can create and have their own song, fast and simple, without understanding anything about music. One photo, and they have a real song — with vocals, lyrics, and a melody that actually matches the moment.

No instruments needed. No musical training. No complicated UI. Just pick a photo, and the magic happens. That's it. That's the whole story.
